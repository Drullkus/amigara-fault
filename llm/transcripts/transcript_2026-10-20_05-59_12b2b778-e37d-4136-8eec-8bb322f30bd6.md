**Why Kinect v2 won**

| Option | Cost | Range | Depth output | Latency | On this Mac |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kinect v2 + adapter | $15 to $45 sensor, $25 to $50 adapter | 0.5 to 4.5 m rated, usable to about 6 m with noise | 512x424 at 30 fps, time of flight | 20 to 60 ms | Unverified, see risk below |
| Kinect v1 (Xbox 360) | $15 to $30 plus $10 12 V cable | 0.8 to 4 m rated, noisy past that | 640x480 at 30 fps, structured light | about 100 ms | Builds on arm64, reported flaky |
| Webcam + mono depth | $0 | Any | Relative depth only, flickers | about 50 to 80 ms | Native Core ML, no driver risk |
| OAK-D Lite | $269 | 0.4 to 8 m | 480p stereo, under 4% error at 3 to 6 m | low | Good, but over budget |
| RealSense D435 used | $134 to $250 | best under 3 m | 848x480 stereo | low | Unofficial since macOS 10.13, unstable |
| Unitree L2 LiDAR | $419 | 30 m | 64k points per second, sparse, not per pixel | low | Over budget and wrong shape |

LiDAR is out: anything per pixel costs several times your budget, and 2D units give one scan line. DIY stereo from two webcams is out too: a short baseline gives poor depth at 3 to 6 m, and calibration plus sync would eat most of your 12 hours.

**Shopping list**

- Xbox One Kinect, model 1520. Sold used for $16 to $46 on eBay this year, so check the sold filter and local listings. Avoid the Xbox 360 one unless you want the backup.
- Kinect v2 PC adapter. Microsoft's $50 original is discontinued. Third-party clones are common on eBay and Amazon, but I could not confirm a 2026 price, so budget $25 to $50. The adapter includes the power brick, which the sensor needs.
- Optional backup: a Kinect v1 model 1414 with a 12 V adapter cable, around $25 total.

**The risk: Kinect v2 on Apple Silicon**

The driver is libfreenect2, and the evidence is mixed. FreenectTD claims Kinect v2 on Apple Silicon works, and a Conan arm64 package exists. But [libfreenect2 issue 1211](https://github.com/OpenKinect/libfreenect2/issues/1211) from August 2026 reports an M4 Mac detecting the sensor and then failing to start with a USB timeout, with no reply yet. Also, neither bytedeco Java preset ships a macOS arm64 jar, so the Kinect must feed the Java server through a sidecar process, which matches the webcam fallback's design. If any Windows or Linux box with USB 3 is reachable, that removes the risk entirely: run the sidecar there and stream to the Mac.
